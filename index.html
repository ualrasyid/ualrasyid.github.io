I'm Rasyid, a 3D generalist working at the intersection of environments, level design, and interactive experiences.

Unreal Engine and Blender are where most of my ideas begin. Sometimes they become worlds. Sometimes they become game-ready systems. And sometimes, a small problem turns into a tool worth sharing.

Whatever the medium, I like making things that feel good to explore — and actually work.
